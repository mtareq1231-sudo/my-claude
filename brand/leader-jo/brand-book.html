هيبة بلا ضجيج

Presence, without noise

Leader Jo is a fashion label from Jordan for men and women. The logo already holds the whole idea. LEADER is the structure: squared letters in navy, built like a sharp jacket. Jo is the signature: a hand-written mark in the colour of the water at Aqaba, and the two letters of Jordan's country code.

Haybah (هيبة) is the word Jordanians use for the presence a real leader brings into a room. The proposed line says the clothes carry that presence without shouting.

ليدر جو علامة أزياء أردنية للرجال والنساء. الشعار يحمل الفكرة كلها: كلمة LEADER هي البنية، حروف مربّعة بلون كحلي مثل سترة مفصّلة بإتقان. وJo هي التوقيع، بخط اليد وبلون مياه العقبة، وهما أيضاً رمز الأردن على الإنترنت.

The idea

Two halves of one mark

الفكرة

نصفان لعلامة واحدة

Read the logo from left to right and you get the brand's character. First the discipline, then the person. Every design decision should serve one of these two halves, and the strongest work holds both.

Leader · the structure

Squared, heavy, navy. It stands for the cut, the fit and the finish: things that are measured, not improvised. Typography, grids, spacing and the navy base all come from here.

البنية: القصّة والمقاس والتشطيب. كل ما هو مدروس وغير مرتجل.

Jo · the signature

Hand-written, light, aqua. It stands for the person wearing the clothes and for home: Amman's stone, the sea at Aqaba, the night over Wadi Rum. Aqua, texture and local detail come from here.

التوقيع: الشخص الذي يرتدي القطعة، والبلد الذي جاءت منه.

Logo

The logo, exactly as it is

الشعار

الشعار كما هو

The logo stays as designed: one fixed lockup, with only its colours changing to suit the background. Use the full-colour version wherever the background allows it, and the one-colour versions for embroidery, foil, embossing and single-ink printing.

The artwork on this page is redrawn from the image you shared, for layout only. The master file (AI, SVG or PDF) replaces it for anything that gets produced.

Clear space
Keep a margin of X on every side, where X is half the height of the L. Nothing else enters that zone.
Where Jo sits
Jo starts at about three quarters of the L's height and hangs below the baseline, right after the R. It never moves or resizes on its own.
Minimum size
Full lockup: 30 mm wide in print, 140 px on screen. Below that the Jo strokes break up, so use the signature mark (min. 8 mm / 32 px).

Don't

Stretch or squash itScale proportionally, always.
Move the JoIt hangs after the R, below the baseline.
Recolour itOnly the versions shown above.
Add effectsNo shadows, outlines or gradients.

Arabic name · proposal

الاسم بالعربية
ليدرجو

Since Arabic leads, shop signs, packaging and Arabic posts will need the name in Arabic next to the logo. This is a companion to the logo, not a change to it. The same logic carries over: square Kufi answers the squared LEADER letters, and Ruqʿah, the everyday handwriting of Arabic, answers the Jo script.

Sketched here in Reem Kufi and Aref Ruqaa. For the final version a calligrapher should draw جو by hand, and ليدر should be drawn to match the weight of LEADER. Please confirm the spelling first.

Colour

Navy and aqua, on Amman stone

الألوان

كحلي وفيروزي على حجر عمّان

The two brand colours come straight from the logo, and there are no others. Neutrals carry everything else, and each colour is named after a place in Jordan. Stone is the default background, navy does the heavy lifting, and aqua is the signature: small amounts, never large fields of text. The warmth comes from photography and words, not from extra colours.

Values were sampled from the logo image, so confirm them against the master file. CMYK values are starting points only; proof them on your actual paper and fabric.

Textures

Three places, drawn in brand colours

الملامس

ثلاثة أماكن بألوان العلامة

Jordan shows up as pattern rather than as souvenir imagery. Each texture is drawn from a real place and uses only the brand palette, so it sits next to the logo without competing with it.

One texture per pieceNever mix two textures on the same bag, post or page.
Logo on a calm fieldPlace the logo on plain colour, never directly on a texture.
No new coloursTextures use only the palette. No sand, no gold, no red.
Typography

Square letters in both scripts

الخطوط

حروف مربّعة باللغتين

Four free Google fonts, two per language, with Arabic always set first and a little larger. The display faces echo the squared, rounded-corner LEADER letters. The text faces are a matched pair, so Arabic and English sit together at the same weight on a tag or a web page.

There is no script typeface. The only hand-written element in the brand is the Jo itself.

Reem KufiDisplay · Arabic
أناقة

A geometric Kufi, the Arabic cousin of squared Latin letters. Headlines, campaign lines, product names. Weights 600–700.

Saira · ExpandedDisplay · English

Squared bowls and soft corners, like the logo. Headings in capitals at 112–125% width, weights 600–700.

Graphic language

Corners and one stroke

اللغة البصرية

الزاوية والخط

Two small devices, both taken from the logo. They make a layout recognisable without repeating the logo everywhere.

الحافة الأماميةالعربية تُقرأ من اليمين إلى اليسار، فتُدوَّر الزوايا اليسرى.
Leading edgeEnglish reads left to right, so the right corners round.

The leading corner

The D and R in LEADER round the corners that face forward and keep the back corners square. Panels, buttons and tags do the same. Arabic layouts flip the rounding to the left, because Arabic reads toward the left.

وصل حديثاً

The signature stroke

A single aqua line in the hand of the Jo, used to underline one word. One per layout, at most. It never becomes a pattern, a frame or a background.

Photography

Real light, places we know

التصوير

ضوء حقيقي وأماكن نعرفها

The pictures should look like Jordan on a clear day: calm men and women in sharp clothes, photographed in places people here recognise. This is where the brand's warmth lives.

Places
Stone stairways in Jabal Amman and Weibdeh, the sandstone of Wadi Rum, the shore at Aqaba in the early morning, quiet courtyards.
أدراج جبل عمّان واللويبدة، صخور وادي رم، شاطئ العقبة صباحاً، أحواش هادئة.
Light
Hard daylight with real shadows. Late afternoon sun on limestone is the house light.
ضوء نهار حقيقي وظلال واضحة. شمس العصر على الحجر الأبيض.
People
Men and women styled as equals: same light, same framing, same respect. Upright, still, looking at the camera. Confidence without posing.
الرجال والنساء بالمكانة نفسها. وقفة ثابتة ونظرة مباشرة، ثقة بلا استعراض.
Casting
Jordan as it is: a range of ages, and women who wear hijab and women who don't. Show looks that work for modest dressers too.
الأردن كما هو: أعمار مختلفة، ومحجبات وغير محجبات، وإطلالات تناسب اللباس المحتشم.
Colour
True whites, cool shadows, natural skin. Navy and aqua arrive through clothes, sky and sea, never through a filter.
ألوان طبيعية. الكحلي والفيروزي يأتيان من الملابس والسماء والبحر، لا من الفلاتر.

Camels-and-dunes clichés, studio cut-outs, heavy filters, stock-photo smiles, and the logo pasted on busy pictures.

Voice

Speak like a leader: briefly

النبرة

نتكلم كالقائد: باختصار

Arabic comes first. Write each language natively and never translate word for word: English is the second version, not the original.

Short sentences

Say it once, in as few words as it takes. Let one strong line stand alone.

Name the thing

The fabric, the cut, the fit. Specifics sound confident; adjectives sound nervous.

Fuṣḥā for facts

Labels, product details and policies in clear Modern Standard Arabic. Social captions can borrow a little Jordanian dialect for warmth.

For him and her

Arabic "you" is gendered. Shared posts use the plural or skip "you"; men's and women's posts use the matching form.
